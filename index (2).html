01 / Profile

About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Cyprus University of Technology, working on the SNAP Project, and a Scientific Collaborator in the Department of Humanities at European University Cyprus. I received my PhD in Linguistics from the University of Cyprus in 2024.

My research interests include theoretical and comparative syntax, morphology, language variation and dialectology, second language acquisition, and typical and atypical language development. My work examines how variation across languages and dialects can inform theories of linguistic structure and development.

02 / Research

Research projects

2026–2027 · Postdoctoral research · Cyprus University of Technology

The SNAP Project

Syntax in Narrative Analysis Productions: Evidence from L1 Cypriot-Greek Children with Developmental Language Disorder

The project investigates complex syntactic structures in personal narratives produced by Cypriot Greek-speaking children with and without Developmental Language Disorder (DLD). It connects linguistic analysis with research on children's language development.

Role: Postdoctoral Researcher and Project Coordinator · Funded through the ΜΕΤΑΔΙΔΑΚΤΩΡ 2025 programme of the Cyprus University of Technology.
